Lineforge support

Privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Published by Kitib Studio

Lineforge is a native Mac and iPad application for creating, checking and exporting electrical single line diagrams. This policy explains how Kitib Studio handles information connected with the app and its support service.

Document and project data

The app does not require an account. Drawings and project data you create or open in Lineforge (including .lfg documents and files you export, such as PDF or DXF) stay in the files and locations you choose on your device or in your own cloud storage when you save or share them there. Lineforge does not operate a server that receives your project data.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of the app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs or tracking.

TestFlight

When you use an Apple TestFlight build, Apple may collect and share beta feedback, crash logs, usage information, device details and build information with the developer. This information is managed through Apple’s TestFlight service and is used to investigate bugs, provide support and improve the app. Read Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

Support messages

If you email support, Kitib Studio receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details, screenshots and issue description. We use it only to answer your request, investigate the issue and improve Lineforge. Please avoid sending confidential project information, client-identifying details or unreleased design data unless you intend to share it for support.

Support correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain support records or meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to records we are required to retain.

Sharing

Kitib Studio does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the relevant service, such as Apple for TestFlight and the email provider used to receive support messages, or where required by law.

Security

Keep your project files in a location you trust and use the security controls available on your device. No method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact Kitib Studio at kitib.studio@icloud.com.
