Support for the single line diagram app for Mac and iPad by Kitib Studio.

Get support

If you need help with Lineforge, email kitib.studio@icloud.com. Include the app version (from About Lineforge), your device and a short description of the issue.

Lineforge works with electrical design files. Please remove confidential project information, client names and site details from screenshots and attachments before you send them.

TestFlight and pre-release builds

TestFlight builds are pre-release software. Features and results may change. Do not rely on a beta build as the sole basis for construction, procurement, operation or submittal work. Engineering outputs must be verified by a competent person before use.
